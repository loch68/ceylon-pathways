Privacy

A short, honest note.

Last updated: November 2026

Ceylon Pathways is a small, owner-run private van service based in Nuwara Eliya, Sri Lanka. This page explains plainly what this website does - and does not do - with your information.

No cookies, no analytics, no tracking

This site is a set of static pages. There is no analytics script, no advertising pixel, no cookie of any kind, no login, no booking engine, no payment processing, no newsletter and no contact form. Nothing you do here is measured, profiled or stored by the website itself.

Hosting

The site is served by Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel keeps standard technical logs - IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time of the request - for security and reliability. We do not use those logs to identify, profile or market to visitors.

When you get in touch

The WhatsApp, call and email links open your own app or mail client. Nothing is sent until you choose to send it. If you do write to us, we use what you share - your name, your dates, where you are staying and your questions - only to reply and to plan your journey. It is not sold, shared for marketing, or added to a mailing list.

WhatsApp and email are operated by third parties, and their own privacy terms apply to messages in transit.

Photographs

All photographs on this site were taken by the owner. Some show guests. If you appear in a photograph here and would prefer it not to be shown, write to us and we will remove it.

Links to other sites

Some links lead elsewhere - Tripadvisor, Google Maps, WhatsApp, and the site's designer, Lochgraphy. Once you follow one of those links, that site's privacy policy applies rather than this one.

Changes

If anything described here changes, this page will be updated and the date above revised.

Questions

Email nimanthausiru@gmail.com or call +94 74 048 9900. Ceylon Pathways, Nuwara Eliya, Sri Lanka.
